Agriculture

Idle land is wasted legacy. We put it to work.

Whether you bought your land from us or already own it, anywhere in Nigeria, our agriculture arm tills the soil, manages the farming, and turns dormant ground into productive, income-generating land.

What We Do

Two paths onto the land.

You don't have to have bought land from Nimi Kings to use our farming service, but if you did, we'll activate it for you as part of the relationship.

Agricultural Land Sales & Activation

Buy agricultural land from us and we'll till the soil and manage it on your behalf, turning a bare plot into productive farmland instead of leaving it idle.

Farming on Land You Already Own

Already have land, anywhere in Nigeria, that wasn't bought from us? We can still till and farm it for you as a standalone service. No prior purchase required.

Soil Tilling & Land Preparation

Clearing, tilling, and preparing raw or overgrown land so it's ready for planting: the groundwork every productive farm depends on.

Farm Management & Harvest Partnership

Ongoing crop management through to harvest, with regular updates, so you can be a hands-off landowner while your land earns.

Tell us where it is and what state it's in, purchased from us or not, and we'll tell you what it can become.
