Our Process

Five stages. Zero surprises.

Every project follows the same structured process — so you always know what's happening, what's next, and what it costs before we break ground.

01

Consultation

Brief review, site assessment, budget scoping, and project feasibility with preliminary cost estimate.

02

Design & Planning

Architectural drawings, structural engineering, material selection, Bills of Quantities, and final cost agreement.

03

Approvals

Planning permits, building approvals, and full regulatory compliance handled with relevant authorities.

04

Build

Site mobilisation and phased construction with milestone check-ins, quality inspections, and regular progress reports.

05

Handover

Final inspection, snagging resolution, full documentation package, and client walkthrough before keys are handed over.
