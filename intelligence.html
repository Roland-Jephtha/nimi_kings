Capabilities

What our intelligence engine does.

Four distinct capabilities — each one reducing risk, increasing confidence, and improving outcomes for every client we work with.

Investment Prediction & Scoring

Algorithmic scoring that combines price history, infrastructure pipeline data, demographic trends, regional development plans, and proximity to economic activity centres — to rank the investment potential of any parcel in our network. Scores updated quarterly.

Fraud & Document Verification

Multi-source cross-referencing of land titles, gazette records, survey plans, and ownership histories — flagging discrepancies, duplicate sales, false documentation, and encumbrances before any financial commitment is made.

Smart Property Matching

A requirement-to-property matching system that takes a client brief — budget, location, use case, hold period — and surfaces the best available options, ranked by relevance score, investment rating, and verified title status.

Construction Intelligence

Real-time project dashboards, material cost benchmarks against current market rates, contractor performance ratings, and milestone tracking linked to payment schedules — so clients can monitor builds without being on site every day.

Our Data

Intelligence is only as good as its data.

We source, clean, and continuously update data from multiple authoritative inputs — so our scoring reflects real conditions, not stale assumptions.

Land Registry Records

Historical title allocations, transfer records, and official ownership chains from state land registries across our operating areas.

Government Gazette Archives

Gazette notices of acquisition, revocation orders, and public use declarations — cross-referenced against every property in the network.

Transaction Price History

Verified sale and lease prices from completed transactions — building a granular area-level price index that improves with every deal.

Infrastructure Pipeline Data

Confirmed road, power, and utilities projects — mapped against land parcel locations to score accessibility and development potential.

Court Litigation Databases

Active and historical property dispute records across relevant jurisdictions — flagged automatically when a queried property is involved.

Construction Cost Benchmarks

Material and labour cost benchmarks updated quarterly from active projects — giving clients a reality check on budgets before a block is laid.
