About NIMI KINGS

Built on the conviction that African property markets deserve better.

NIMI KINGS is an integrated real estate, construction, and AI intelligence company operating across Nigeria. We exist to make property transactions safer, buildings better, and investment decisions more precise.

Our Story

Why we exist.

NIMI KINGS was founded with a clear conviction: that the Nigerian property market — and by extension, African property markets — had a trust problem more than a capital problem.

Too many buyers have lost money to unverified land. Too many construction projects have stalled due to poor management. Too many investment decisions have been made on gut feel rather than data. We exist to change each of those things simultaneously.

Our three-pillar model — Real Estate, Construction, and AI Intelligence — is not three separate businesses. It is one integrated approach. The intelligence layer informs every property we sell and every build we manage. The construction capability allows us to add value to land we help clients acquire. The real estate pipeline feeds the intelligence engine with real transaction data that improves our scoring over time.

We operate primarily across Lagos, Nasarawa, Abuja outskirts (FCT), and Kogi State — areas where land values are rapidly shifting and where the risk of misinformation is highest. We chose these corridors because that is where the need is greatest, and where our team has the deepest operational familiarity.

Our Values

What we actually believe.

Not mission statement language. These are the specific positions that shape how we work, who we work with, and what we refuse to compromise on.

01

Trust is earned transaction by transaction.

We do not list, sell, or manage any property we have not independently verified. We have turned away business rather than take on unverified inventory. That position protects clients, and it builds the kind of reputation that survives long term in a market where trust is the scarcest commodity.

02

Intelligence is infrastructure.

We believe data and AI are not add-ons to property — they are the foundation every reliable transaction should be built on. Our intelligence engine is not a marketing tool. It is a risk reduction system, and we use it on every single deal, not just the ones where clients ask for it.

03

Execution is the only credibility that matters.

The Nigerian market has many organisations that speak well. Credibility here is earned by delivery — on-time, on-budget, and fully documented. We measure ourselves by what we complete, not what we announce. Every project, every transaction, every report is a test of that commitment.
