Why it matters

Unverified land is Nigeria's biggest investment risk.

Title fraud, multiple sales of the same parcel, and litigation-encumbered land destroy wealth every year across Nigeria. We exist to change that — one verified transaction at a time.

Every property we handle undergoes a rigorous 5-point verification protocol: title history, physical boundary confirmation, gazette cross-check, litigation search, and infrastructure status assessment.
